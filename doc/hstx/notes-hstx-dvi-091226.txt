HSTX stands for High-Speed Serial Transmit, a new hardware peripheral introduced on the Raspberry Pi 
RP2350 microcontroller. [1] (https://www.digikey.com/en/maker/tutorials/2025/what-is-the-rp2350-high-speed-transmit-interface-hstx), [2] (https://www.cnx-software.com/2024/08/15/raspberry-pi-rp2350-hstx-high-speed-serial-transmit-interface/)

What is HSTX?
High-Speed Serial Output: It streams data from the system clock domain to up to eight GPIO pins 
(GPIO 12 through 19) without needing much help from the main CPU

Independent Clock: It runs in its own clock domain at up to 150 MHz.

Double Data Rate (DDR): It transmits two bits per pin and clock cycle, reaching an effective 
rate of 300 Mb/s per pin (or 2.4 Gb/s total across all eight pins)

What is it used for?
Video Generation: It creates high-speed video signals like DVI and VGA directly from the microcontroller.

Fast Data Streaming: It handles high-speed memory interfacing and fast audio transmission with 
near-zero CPU overhead.
